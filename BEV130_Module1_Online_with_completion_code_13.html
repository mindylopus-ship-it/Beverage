Every beverage has a story.

Whether it's a bottle of wine enjoyed at a restaurant, a craft beer picked up at a local store, a spirit poured into a cocktail, or a ready-to-drink can grabbed from a cooler, each product traveled through a network of businesses and professionals before it ever reached you.

The beverage industry is a dynamic ecosystem spanning agriculture, manufacturing, distribution, retail, hospitality, marketing, logistics, and entrepreneurship. Producers transform raw materials into products. Distributors move those products through the marketplace. Retailers and hospitality businesses create the consumer experience. In this module you'll explore how those pieces fit together — and where you might fit into the picture.

By the end of this online session, you'll be able to
  • Define the major sectors of the beverage industry
  • Explain the role of production, distribution, and retail/hospitality
  • Identify career opportunities throughout the beverage supply chain
  • Describe how products move from producer to consumer
  • Analyze the relationship between industry stakeholders

Module Summary

The beverage industry is a complex ecosystem composed of production, distribution, and retail/hospitality sectors. Each plays a critical role in moving products from raw materials to consumers. Understanding these relationships provides the foundation for future study of beverage production, sales, service, marketing, and entrepreneurship.

Key Takeaways

  • The beverage industry is an interconnected ecosystem, not a single business.
  • Production, distribution, and retail/hospitality form the foundation of the industry.
  • Many career paths exist beyond brewing, winemaking, and bartending.
  • Understanding the supply chain explains how products actually reach consumers.
  • Every organization in the industry contributes to the customer experience.
